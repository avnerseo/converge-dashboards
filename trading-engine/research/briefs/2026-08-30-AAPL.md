## Bull case

Demand is not the problem and the data says so: iPhone +22% delivered, +13%
shipments per IDC, record iOS share into a collapsing market. The constraint is
a component Apple does not make — and Apple is attacking it on three fronts at
once (price increases, leasing to protect volume, CXMT qualification). The base
has held for a month and Friday closed at its high, with RSI at 57 rather than
exhausted. Price is above both the 50- and 200-day averages. The product wave
lands inside the window.

## Bear case

At $319.70 the stock sits **+1.5% from the $324.45 consensus target** pulled
from the data layer — the market has already paid for the recovery. PE 36.1,
forward PE 33.0, PEG 2.54, price/book 42.7. The margin headwind is not a
one-quarter event: management guided 50% → 47–48% and called it a hundred-year
flood, and the fix Apple is reaching for is being blocked in Washington. On the
horizon that matters, AAPL is still **−10.94% against SPY over 21 sessions**.
Jefferies, holding a $263.66 target, is not a fringe voice.

## Key risks

1. **CEO transition on 2026-09-01 — two days into the window.** John Ternus
   replaces Tim Cook. Idiosyncratic, unhedgeable, and with no directional sign.
2. **Memory prices.** A further spike hits margin again; relief lifts the stock.
   Neither is forecastable from anything pulled here.
3. **The CXMT political fight.** An adverse ruling closes Apple's cheapest fix.
4. **Benchmark contamination.** AAPL is one of the largest weights in SPY, so

**Why NO-EDGE, stated plainly.**

The bull case rests on a 10-day price move. That is the weakest evidence there
is, and it is exactly the pattern-hypothesis class that died in this project's
own ten rejections and that `LITSCAN_RESULT_2026-08-29.md` closed as a family
one day ago. Predicting UP because a stock rose for ten days would be the same
error this brief format exists to prevent.

The bear case rests on valuation and a margin headwind that management
announced on a public call four weeks ago. "Expensive" is not a 21-day
catalyst, and a known headwind is a priced headwind.

The two cases cancel, and the analyst spread — **$263.66 to $400 on the same
company in the same month** — is what informed disagreement looks like when
nobody has an edge.
